# Community conduct

Discuss ideas and evidence respectfully. Welcome questions from newcomers and contributors from different disciplines. Provide actionable criticism of code or methods, not attacks on people. Harassment, discrimination, threats, disclosure of private information, and knowingly fabricated research results are not acceptable.

This applies to issues, pull requests and project discussions. Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and restrict participation proportionately; explain moderation decisions when doing so does not expose private information.

Report concerns privately to the corresponding author, Yonghao Wang, at yonghao2025@hnu.edu.cn. Include only the information needed to understand the concern. Do not post private reports publicly. Maintainers should recuse themselves from handling reports in which they have a conflict of interest.
